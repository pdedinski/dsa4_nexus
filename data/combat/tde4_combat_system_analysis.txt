================================================================================
TDE 4.1 / DSA 4.1 COMBAT SYSTEM ANALYSIS
================================================================================
Sources: DSA 4.1 Basisregelwerk (BRW) pp. 99-168
         Wege des Schwertes (WdS) pp. 58-107

This document is structured similarly to tde_dsa41_character_generation_summary.txt.
It describes the full combat system algorithmically — suitable for use as a
reference when implementing combat logic or building a digital assistant.

================================================================================
SECTION 1 — COMBAT OVERVIEW & SEQUENCE
================================================================================

A combat encounter in TDE 4.1 proceeds in ROUNDS. Each round represents roughly
3 seconds of in-fiction time and consists of these phases:

  1. INITIATIVE PHASE
     - Every participant rolls initiative (see Section 2).
     - Act in order from highest to lowest. Ties broken by the rule below.

  2. ACTION PHASE (in initiative order)
     Each combatant, on their turn, has ONE ACTION to spend:
       a) ATTACK ACTION  — strike, maneuver, charge, etc.
       b) FREE ACTION    — declared before or after an attack (e.g. Call,
                          Defensive Posture, Disarm)
       c) SPECIAL ACTIONS — flee, mount/dismount, draw weapon, pick up item,
                           call for help, etc.

     After an attacker resolves an ATTACK ACTION:
       - The TARGET may use a DEFENSE ACTION: Parry, Shield Parry, Dodge.
       - Defenses are not limited to the defender's turn — they respond
         to incoming attacks at any point in the round.
       - A defender can only make ONE defense per incoming attack.
       - Additional attackers increase defense difficulty (see Sec 9).

  3. END OF ROUND
     - Ongoing effects tick (bleed, poison, fire, etc.).
     - Initiative is re-rolled only if the GM specifies; otherwise the order
       from round 1 continues.

KEY STATS USED IN COMBAT
  AT  — Attack Value    (base = round((CO+AG+ST)/5) + melee talent TP allocation)
  PA  — Parry Value     (base = round((IN+AG+ST)/5) + melee talent TP allocation)
  FK  — Ranged Value    (base = round((IN+AG+ST)/5) = baseBRV + ranged talent TP)
  INI — Initiative      (see Section 2)
  TP  — Talent Prowess  (combat talent level, 0–21+ during creation)
  WM  — Weapon Modifier (AT/PA modifiers on the weapon entry)
  BE  — Encumbrance     (armor BE stacked; reduces AT/PA via EEC formula)
  RS  — Armor Rating    (damage reduction applied to hits)
  DP  — Damage Points   (raw damage dealt by a weapon hit)
  VP  — Life Points     (derived: ceil((CN+CN+ST)/2) + racial/culture/profession mods)
  EP  — Endurance       (derived: ceil((CO+CN+AG)/2) + modifiers)
  KO  — Constitution    (base attribute; drives wound threshold)

================================================================================
SECTION 2 — INITIATIVE
================================================================================

FORMULA (BRW p. 101):
  INI = round((CO + CO + IN + AG) / 5) + racial INI modifier + die roll

DIE ROLL:
  Roll 1d6 at the start of EACH round (some weapon/armor mods apply).
  Add the die result to the base INI formula above.

  Final INI = base INI + 1d6 + any situational modifiers

MODIFIERS TO INI (applied each round):
  Weapon ini_modifier   — heavy two-handed weapons typically -1 to -3
  Armor ini_modifier    — heavier armor typically -1 to -4
  Surprised combatant   — acts LAST in the first round of an ambush
  Defensive Posture SA  — no penalty from WdS
  Charge maneuver       — +1 INI on the charge round (WdS p. 65)

TIEBREAKER (BRW p. 101):
  1) Higher CO wins.
  2) If still tied: simultaneous, both act at the same moment.

DELAYED ACTION:
  A combatant may choose to DELAY their turn. They may interrupt at any later
  point in the round, acting before the next combatant in the order.
  If they do not act before the round ends, their action is lost.

================================================================================
SECTION 3 — ATTACK RESOLUTION
================================================================================

MELEE ATTACK ROLL (BRW pp. 102-103):
  Roll 1d20. If result ≤ finalAT → HIT. If result > finalAT → MISS.

  finalAT = baseAT + talentTPforAT + weapon_AT_modifier - eBE_AT_penalty
            + situational modifiers

  where:
    baseAT           = round((CO+AG+ST)/5)
    talentTPforAT    = allocated portion of combat talent TP (see Section 3a)
    weapon_AT_modifier = weapon's at_modifier field
    eBE_AT_penalty   = floor(effectiveBE / 2) for melee (Section 8)

RANGED ATTACK ROLL (BRW pp. 152-155):
  Roll 1d20. If result ≤ finalFK → HIT.

  finalFK = baseFKValue + talentTP - eBE_penalty
            + range_band_FK_modifier + situational modifiers

  where baseFKValue = round((IN+AG+ST)/5)  [= baseBRV in generator terms]
  Range band FK modifiers (BRW p. 153):
    sehr_nah    (very close): FK -2
    nah         (close):      FK +0
    mittel      (medium):     FK +4
    weit        (far):        FK +8
    extrem_weit (extreme):    FK +12

CRITICAL HIT (BRW p. 103):
  If unmodified die = 1  → possible critical. Roll 1d20 again.
    If second roll also ≤ finalAT → CONFIRMED CRITICAL:
      Damage is doubled (roll twice, sum both, OR roll once × 2; GM convention).
      Target does NOT get a defense action.
    If second roll > finalAT → normal hit. Target may defend normally.

FUMBLE (BRW p. 103):
  If unmodified die = 20 → FUMBLE. Roll 1d20 on fumble table.
  Common fumble results: weapon dropped, attacker falls, weapon breaks, etc.
  (Fumble table BRW p. 108.)

SITUATIONAL AT MODIFIERS:
  Fighting from behind        +4 AT
  Target prone / on ground    +4 AT (melee), -4 AT (ranged)
  Target unaware of attack    automatic hit or +4 AT (GM discretion)
  Dim lighting                -2 AT
  Darkness                    -6 AT (or automatic miss if completely blind)
  Called shot to a body part  -4 AT (head: -8 AT), special effect on hit

--- Section 3a — MELEE TP SPLIT (AT vs PA) ---

Combat talent TP is split between AT and PA allocation (not spent separately):
  at_allocated = floor(TP / 2)
  pa_allocated = TP - at_allocated   [i.e., ceil(TP/2)]

  Bias adjustment (±2 TP shift before clamp):
    If the selected weapon's net (PA_mod - AT_mod) > 0 → "offensive" bias:
      at_allocated += 2  (weapon favors AT due to low PA WM)
    If net < 0 → "defensive" bias:
      pa_allocated += 2  (weapon favors defense via high PA WM)
    If net = 0 → use character concept bias (offensive/defensive/balanced)

  Clamp: |at_allocated - pa_allocated| ≤ 5

  Final melee AT = baseAT + at_allocated + weapon_AT_modifier - eBE_AT
  Final melee PA = basePA + pa_allocated + weapon_PA_modifier - eBE_PA

SHIELD MODIFIER (stacks with weapon, applies to all melee with that hand):
  Shield AT modifier (negative) reduces AT
  Shield PA modifier (positive) raises PA
  Shield mods DO NOT apply when wielding two-handed weapons.
  Chain weapons IGNORE the shield PA bonus.

================================================================================
SECTION 4 — DEFENSE RESOLUTION
================================================================================

After a successful hit, the defender may choose ONE defense:

  a) PARRY (PA roll) — active block with weapon
     Roll 1d20. If result ≤ finalPA → PARRIED.
     finalPA = basePA + talentTPforPA + weapon_PA_modifier - eBE_PA_penalty
     Restrictions:
       - Cannot parry chain weapons with daggers.
       - Cannot parry chain/two-handed blunt with fencing weapons.
       - Cannot parry two-handed swords with daggers or fencing weapons.
       - Shields add their PA modifier to any parry in the same hand.

  b) SHIELD PARRY — parry with a shield (independent of main-hand weapon)
     Roll against shield PA only (weapon talent AT/PA not involved).
     Shield PA = basePA + shield_PA_modifier - eBE_PA_penalty
     Chain weapons IGNORE this PA bonus.

  c) DODGE (Ausweichen)
     Roll 1d20 vs. Dodge value.
     Dodge = (AG + GE + KK) / 5, rounded (BRW p. 104).
     [Note: GE = Agility (already reflected in AG in this system; BRW uses it.]
     Penalties:
       - Each subsequent dodge attempt in the same round: -3 cumulative.
       - Cannot dodge if already prone (GM discretion).

FAILED DEFENSE:
  If no defense succeeds (or attacker confirms critical), proceed to damage.

CRITICAL PARRY (BRW p. 104):
  Unmodified PA die = 1 → possible critical. Roll again.
  If confirmed: attacker's weapon may be trapped/disarmed (STR contest).

PARRY FUMBLE:
  Unmodified PA die = 20 → defender's weapon may drop or break.

================================================================================
SECTION 5 — DAMAGE RESOLUTION
================================================================================

DAMAGE CALCULATION (BRW pp. 104-105):
  DP (raw) = weapon damage roll (e.g., 1d6+4) + applicable bonuses
  DP (effective) = max(0, DP - RS)   where RS is the armor's armor rating

  RS = sum of body armor RS + helmet RS (for head hits) + limb RS (for limb hits)
  (Overlapping armor is additive unless special rules apply.)

WOUND THRESHOLD:
  If effective DP ≥ KO/2 (rounded down):
    → WOUND inflicted
    Target suffers a wound marker.
    First wound:  -1 AT, -1 PA, -1 to all checks
    Second wound: -2 AT, -2 PA, -2 to all checks (cumulative from first)
    Each subsequent wound: additional -1 per wound marker
  If effective DP = 0: hit absorbed by armor, no damage.

STUN (Betäubung):
  If effective DP ≥ KO/2 AND weapon is blunt (Blunt Blow or blunt weapon):
    → Target must pass a KO check (1d20 ≤ KO) or be stunned.
    Stunned: cannot act for 1d3 rounds, PA only at -4.

KNOCKDOWN:
  Certain maneuvers (Sweeping Blow, etc.) may cause knockdown.
  Prone target: +4 AT for attackers, standing up costs a full action.

ENDURANCE VS LIFE POINTS:
  Brawling / unarmed DP type (A) = half to LP, half to EP.
  Normal weapon DP → full to LP.
  EP (Endurance) loss from fatigue, exertion, unarmed hits, some spells.
  VP (Life Points) loss from weapon hits after armor.

DEATH & INCAPACITATION:
  VP ≤ 0  → unconscious and dying (1 additional VP lost per round without help)
  VP = -VP_max → dead
  EP ≤ 0  → exhausted, collapses (unconscious)

================================================================================
SECTION 6 — THE CALL (Der Ansage)
================================================================================

Source: WdS pp. 58-60

Many maneuvers allow the attacker to voluntarily reduce their own AT or PA
(an "ante") to achieve a special effect. This self-chosen penalty is the CALL.

RULES:
  - The Call is declared BEFORE the die roll.
  - Total penalty (maneuver base + Called points) must NOT exceed:
      a) The TP of the weapon talent used, AND
      b) The relevant AT or PA value itself.
  - The declared Call must be a whole number ≥ 1.
  - For OFFENSIVE maneuvers: Call reduces AT. Cap = min(TP, finalAT).
  - For DEFENSIVE maneuvers: Call reduces PA. Cap = min(TP, finalPA).

FAILED CALL PENALTY:
  If a maneuver with a Call FAILS (AT or PA roll fails):
    → Suffer the full Called value as a penalty to ALL subsequent rolls
      until the fighter uses their next valid Attack or Parry action.
    → This includes Free Actions requiring tests during that time.
    → Once the next Attack or Parry is resolved, the penalty clears.

BLADE DANCER SPECIAL ABILITY (WdS p. 60):
  Reduces the failed-Call penalty to HALF (rounded down).

EXAMPLES (BRW p. 60):
  Call of 4 on a Feint: AT reduced by 4 for that roll.
  If it fails → all actions penalized by 4 until next AT/PA action.
  With Blade Dancer: penalty is only 2.

================================================================================
SECTION 7 — MANEUVER CATALOGUE SUMMARY
================================================================================

Each maneuver entry has: Type | Base Penalty | SA Required | Compatible Talents

--- ATTACK ACTIONS ---

Standard Attack
  Penalty: none | SA: no | Talents: all
  Basic offensive action. Roll AT; if successful target may defend.

Feint
  Penalty: AT -(Call, min 1) | SA: no | Talents: melee only
  On success: defender's PA reduced by (Call × 2) for that defense.
  The Call caps at min(TP, finalAT).

Targeted Attack (Called Shot)
  Penalty: AT -4 (body), -8 (head/neck), -2 (limbs) | SA: no
  Hit to specific location. Effects vary by location hit.
  Head: chance of stun/knockdown. Limb: reduced effectiveness of that limb.

Sweeping Blow
  Penalty: AT -4 | SA: no | Talents: axes, two-handed, staves, etc.
  On hit: target must pass KK check (BRW: KK/2) or fall prone.

Blade Storm (Klingensturm)
  Penalty: each AT = AV/2+2 | SA: yes (Blade Storm SA)
  Split one Attack Action between TWO opponents.
  Max EC 4. No shield. Cannot combine with another maneuver.
  With Combat Awareness: distribute freely (min 6 each).
  With Blade Dancer: split among up to THREE opponents.

Disarming Strike
  Penalty: AT -4 | SA: no | Talents: melee (most)
  On hit: instead of damage, attempt disarm (opposed STR check).

Charge (Angriff mit Anlauf)
  Bonus: +2 AT on charge round, +1 INI | Cost: must move at least 3 paces
  Cannot combine with most other maneuvers.

Blunt Blow (Stumpfer Schlag)
  Penalty: AT -2 (club/staff), -4 (axe/mace flat), -8 (sword flat)
  On hit: triggers stun check regardless of DP.

Thrust (Stoß)  [spears, infantry weapons, staves]
  Penalty: AT -(Call) | Effect: TP shifts toward distance-class effects.
  Targets at incorrect range for weapon suffer additional penalties.

Power Attack / All-Out Attack
  Penalty: PA -4 until next turn | Bonus: AT +4, +1d6 DP if hit
  Cannot combine with Call (the PA penalty is the built-in cost).

--- DEFENSE ACTIONS ---

Standard Parry
  No penalty | SA: no | Talents: all melee capable of PA
  Roll 1d20 ≤ PA. Success = hit negated.

Evasive Maneuver (Ausweichen)
  Roll Dodge value | SA: no | Talents: n/a
  Each subsequent dodge this round: -3 cumulative.

Defensive Posture (Verteidigungsposition)
  Free Action (declared before combat or at start of round).
  +2 PA for the entire round, -2 AT for the entire round.
  With Defensive Posture SA (WdS): +4 PA, -2 AT.

Full Defense (Volle Abwehr)
  Spend the entire action on defense.
  +4 PA for the round. No AT action possible.
  With appropriate SA: may parry multiple attacks.

Parrying Blade (Parierdolch / Sai)
  Off-hand parrying weapon grants its PA modifier as additional PA.
  Counts as a second defense per incoming attack (must be declared).

--- FREE ACTIONS ---

Free actions may be combined with an Attack or Defense action:

Call Declaration    — declare Call amount before rolling
Defensive Posture   — enter/exit defensive stance
Readied Weapon      — quick-draw a prepared weapon (not sheathed)
Taunting            — attempt to provoke opponent (social check)
Combat Awareness    — activate SA effect (if available)
Observation         — note attacker position for next round's modifier

--- SPECIAL SITUATIONS ---

Fighting Blind (Darkness)
  -6 AT and -6 PA. Ranged attacks: near-impossible (-8 FK).

Prone Target
  Attacker: +4 AT (melee). Ranged: no modifier.
  Prone defender: PA -4. Standing from prone: 1 full action.

Fighting in Restricted Space
  AT -2 to -4 depending on space. Long weapons suffer more.

Two-Weapon Fighting (Zwei-Waffen-Kampf)
  Off-hand weapon may be used for ONE attack per round.
  Off-hand AT: -4 (or -2 with Two-Weapon Fighting SA).
  Main hand AT: -2 (or no penalty with Two-Weapon Fighting SA).
  Off-hand cannot be used for PA in the same round as attack.
  Exception: parrying weapons (see Parrying Blade above).

Grappling (Ringen)
  Uses Wrestling talent. On hit: grapple begins.
  Each round: opposed TP rolls. Winner may deal 1d6(A) or throw.
  Choke: 1d6+1 DP per round if maintained; target must pass KO check.
  Throw: grappler chooses to slam opponent; fall damage applies.

================================================================================
SECTION 8 — ENCUMBRANCE IN COMBAT
================================================================================

Source: BRW pp. 105-106

BE (Behinderung) = Encumbrance value listed on each armor piece.

STACKING ARMOR BE:
  Body armor BE is listed per piece. Stack additively.
  Exception: Quilted Underclothing does NOT add its BE to metal armor worn
  over it. It only adds when worn alone.

EC (Effective Encumbrance after talent reduction):
  Each combat talent has an EEC (Effective Encumbrance threshold).
  Format in data: "EC-4" means talent reduces total BE by 4.

  eBE = max(0, totalBE - talent_EEC_reduction)

PENALTY APPLICATION:
  Melee AT penalty:  floor(eBE / 2)
  Melee PA penalty:  ceil(eBE / 2)   [odd remainder goes to PA]
  Ranged FK penalty: full eBE (not split)
  Jousting AT penalty: full eBE

EXAMPLE:
  Character wears Full Chain (BE 5) + Open Helm (BE 1) = total BE 6.
  Combat talent = Swords (EEC "EC-2") → eBE = max(0, 6-2) = 4.
  AT penalty = floor(4/2) = 2.  PA penalty = ceil(4/2) = 2.

SWIMMING, BALANCE, CLIMBING penalties from armor:
  Armor BE applies fully to these physical checks (not reduced by combat talent).

================================================================================
SECTION 9 — FIGHTING MULTIPLE OPPONENTS
================================================================================

Source: BRW p. 106

When a defender faces multiple simultaneous attackers:
  Each attacker beyond the first imposes -1 PA on ALL parries for the round.
  Two attackers:  defender PA -1
  Three attackers: defender PA -2
  Four attackers:  defender PA -3
  (Cap at -5 for 6+ attackers. GM may rule differently.)

The penalty does NOT reduce AT of the defender — only their ability to defend.

FLANKING (BRW p. 106):
  Attacking from behind or a flank: +4 AT for the flanking attacker.
  Defender cannot use PA against attacks they cannot see.
  Defender may Dodge instead (no facing restriction on Dodge).

TEAM TACTICS:
  Combatants may attempt to coordinate.
  A flanking ally who succeeds at a Tactics/Military Doctrine check (WdS)
  may give the primary attacker +2 AT for one round.

================================================================================
SECTION 10 — MOUNTED COMBAT
================================================================================

Source: WdS pp. 100-107

PREREQUISITES:
  Rider must have Riding talent (or applicable animal skill).
  Combat while mounted: AT and PA at -2 unless Mounted Combat SA is known.

MOUNTED ATTACK BONUSES:
  Attacking on foot from horseback (disadvantaged: horse not fully trained):
    Standard modifiers apply.
  Charge with couched lance (Lanzenreiten talent):
    Damage multiplied by mount speed factor.
    Speed factor = (mount movement / 10) rounded, minimum ×2.
    Lance cannot be used to parry. AT uses Jousting talent only.

DISTANCE CLASSES FOR MOUNTED COMBAT (WdS p. 101):
  Reaching opponents at wrong distance class: -2 AT per class off.
  Lance at very close range: cannot be used offensively.
  Short weapons (daggers) at long range: cannot strike.

BREAKING OFF MOUNTED COMBAT:
  Mounted combatant may disengage freely if mount's speed > opponent's speed.
  On foot opponent: pursuit requires Movement check.

CHARGING THROUGH INFANTRY:
  Warhorse or destrier vs. infantry: each figure in the path must pass a
  KK check or be knocked down (BRW horse charge rules, WdS p. 104).

DISMOUNTING:
  Voluntary: 1 full action.
  Forced (from knockdown/lance hit): opposed KK check vs. DP dealt.
  Fall damage from involuntary dismount: 1d6 per 2m height (horse = ~1.5m).

SECTION 11 — OPTIONAL RULES (Wege des Schwertes)
================================================================================

Source: WdS pp. 78-86

These are explicitly marked as optional (optionale Regeln) in WdS.

LOCATION HITS (Trefferzone, WdS p. 78):
  Roll 1d20 on location table after a hit is confirmed.
  Location affects which armor RS applies and wound effects.
  d20 Location Table (approximate):
    1-5:   Torso          (full body armor RS)
    6-8:   Right arm      (arm armor RS, or 0 if none)
    9-11:  Left arm       (arm armor RS, or 0 if none)
    12-14: Right leg      (leg armor RS, or 0 if none)
    15-17: Left leg       (leg armor RS, or 0 if none)
    18-19: Head           (helmet RS, or 0 if none; wound = stun check)
    20:    Called location (attacker chooses; -4 AT already paid if Called Shot)

WEAPON BREAKAGE (WdS p. 79):
  On a fumble, roll for weapon quality. Poor weapons may break.
  On a critical parry, attacker's weapon trapped; STR contest for disarm/break.

ARMOR DAMAGE (WdS p. 80):
  After a wound, RS of struck armor piece may be permanently reduced.
  Roll: if original DP ≥ RS of that piece, RS -1 (repaired with smithing skill).

FATIGUE IN COMBAT (WdS p. 81):
  Every 3 rounds of active combat: 1 EP lost.
  At EP < 10%: -2 AT, -2 PA.
  At EP = 0: collapse (unconscious).

CALLED SHOTS (Gezielter Stoß, WdS p. 83):
  Extended rules for targeting specific body parts with precise effects:
    Throat: on wound → voice loss, choking damage
    Hand/wrist: on wound → weapon drop (KK check to retain)
    Eye: on wound → blindness (one eye), -3 AT permanently if not healed

TERRAIN MODIFIERS (WdS p. 85):
  Mud/ice: -2 AT and -2 PA, or risk of slipping (AG check)
  Stairs/elevation: +1 AT for higher combatant, -1 for lower
  Water (waist-deep): AT/PA halved (round down); Dodge impossible

PSYCHOLOGICAL EFFECTS / MORALE (WdS p. 86):
  Optional fear/morale checks using IN or MU attributes.
  Triggered by: outnumbered ≥3:1, facing supernatural opponents,
                seeing allies slain, etc.
  Failed check: -2 AT/-2 PA (shaken) or flee (routed).

SECTION 12 — QUICK REFERENCE: KEY FORMULAS
================================================================================

  baseAT         = round((CO + AG + ST) / 5)
  basePA         = round((IN + AG + ST) / 5)
  baseFKValue    = round((IN + AG + ST) / 5)   [= baseBRV]
  baseINI        = round((CO + CO + IN + AG) / 5) + racial INI mod
  Initiative     = baseINI + 1d6 + weapon_INI_mod + armor_INI_mod

  Final AT       = baseAT + at_alloc + weapon_AT_mod + shield_AT_mod - eBE_AT
  Final PA       = basePA + pa_alloc + weapon_PA_mod + shield_PA_mod - eBE_PA
  Final FK       = baseFKValue + talent_TP + weapon_AT_mod - eBE + range_mod

  eBE            = max(0, totalBE - talent_EEC)
  AT eBE penalty = floor(eBE / 2)
  PA eBE penalty = ceil(eBE / 2)

  Wound threshold = effective DP ≥ floor(KO / 2)

  WM (Weapon Modifier):
    at_modifier on weapon: adds to AT
    pa_modifier on weapon: adds to PA (may be negative for offensive weapons)

  TP Split (melee):
    at_alloc = floor(TP / 2)  [+ or - 2 based on weapon WM bias, clamped]
    pa_alloc = TP - at_alloc

SECTION 13 — GENERATOR COMPATIBILITY NOTES
================================================================================

These notes describe how the combat system maps to the DSA Nexus character
generator (lib/character/generator.ts and related files).

TALENT POOL (combat_talents.json):
  Combat talents are separate from non-combat talents in the generator.
  TGP (Talent Generation Points) are shared across all talent groups.
  The generator biases TGP spending toward combat talents that match
  the player's selected weapons (via WEAPON_TALENT_BOOST constant).

MELEE TP SPLIT:
  The generator calls allocateMeleeCombatTp(tp, bias) from meleeTpAllocation.ts.
  The split result (allocatedAT, allocatedPA) is stored in combatMelee[].
  The final AT and PA shown on the sheet are: baseAT + allocatedAT and
  basePA + allocatedPA (before weapon modifiers and encumbrance).

WEAPON MODIFIERS AT DISPLAY TIME:
  Applied in loadoutCombatValues.ts → computeLoadoutWeaponLine().
  Final AT = baseAT + at_alloc + weapon.at_modifier + shield.at_mod - eBE_AT.
  Final PA = basePA + pa_alloc + weapon.pa_modifier + shield.pa_mod - eBE_PA.

SHIELD MODIFIERS:
  Shields stored in armorData (armor.json, category="shield").
  The at_modifier and pa_modifier fields on shield entries are summed in
  sumShieldModifiers() and applied to melee combat lines.
  Two-handed weapon talents (MELEE_NO_SHIELD_TALENTS set) ignore shield mods.

ENCUMBRANCE FLOW:
  Generator does not apply eBE — it stores talent TP splits.
  eBE application happens in loadoutCombatValues.ts at display time,
  using the talent's eec field from combat_talents.json.

RANGED TALENTS:
  All TP goes to AT (no PA). Ranged combat does not use PA.
  combatRanged[] stores: talentId, talentName, tp, finalAT.
  finalAT = baseBRV + tp (generator). Weapon AT modifier applied at display.

JOUSTING:
  Similar to ranged (AT only). Uses baseAT + tp, not baseBRV.
  Covered in computeLoadoutWeaponLine() with combatType === "jousting".

SHIELDS AS WEAPONS:
  Shields appear in weapons.json with combat_talent = null and is_shield = true.
  Shield parry is not a Kampftechnik (combat talent): it uses base PA + shield weapon WM
  + encumbrance + PA bonuses from the Special Abilities Off-hand Fighting (+1),
  Shield Fighting I (+2), and Shield Fighting II (+2), summed from the character sheet.
  loadoutCombatValues.ts treats these rows in computeLoadoutWeaponLine() separately from
  melee-talent weapons. Companion shield WM (from another shield weapon in the loadout or
  from armor rows with category shield) is added to one-handed melee weapon lines when
  applicable. Legacy saved loadouts may still have combat_talent "shields" or omit is_shield;
  known shield ids are detected as a fallback.
