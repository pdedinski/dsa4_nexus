THE DARK EYE / DAS SCHWARZE AUGE 4.x
GERMAN-ENGLISH ABBREVIATIONS AND RULES TERMINOLOGY
======================================================================================================================

Sources used
------------
1. Das Schwarze Auge: Basisregelwerk, Hardcover-Ausgabe 2008 (German DSA 4.1).
2. The Dark Eye: Basic Rules, FanPro PDF version 1.0, May 2004 (English fourth-edition rules).

Scope and edition warning
-------------------------
The two books are closely related but are NOT exact page-for-page editions of the same text.
The German 2008 book is a later DSA 4.1 revision; the English book is an earlier fourth-edition
translation. Some terms, categories, and abbreviations therefore have no exact counterpart.

The English column below follows the terminology actually used by the English Basic Rules.
Where the English book has no stable abbreviation, the table says so rather than inventing one.
Recommended modern shorthand is shown only when it is useful and clearly labelled.

Most important rule for reading mixed-language material:
  German TP = Trefferpunkte = English HP (Hit Points).
  English TP = Talent Prowess = German TaW (Talentwert).
  German SP = Schadenspunkte = English DP (Damage Points).
  English SP = Spell Prowess = German ZfW (Zauberfertigkeitswert).

10. RECOMMENDED ENGLISH STAT-BLOCK ORDER
======================================================================================================================
For consistent English-language DSA/TDE 4.1 notes, use this order:

  Attributes: CO, CL, IN, CH, DE, AG, CN, ST
  Resources:  VP, EP, ASP
  Defense:    RM, AR, EC/EEC
  Combat:     INI, AT, PA, RV, HP, HP/ST, WM, BP, DC, SD
  Talents:    TP (Talent Prowess); remaining points = TP*
  Spells:     SP (Spell Prowess); remaining points = SP*
  Damage:     HP before armor; DP after armor

Example conversion
------------------
German:  MU 14, KL 11, IN 13, CH 10, FF 12, GE 14, KO 15, KK 16
         LE 38, AU 36, MR 5, RS 7, BE 4, AT 17, PA 14, TP 1W6+5

English: CO 14, CL 11, IN 13, CH 10, DE 12, AG 14, CN 15, ST 16
         VP 38, EP 36, RM 5, AR 7, EC 4, AT 17, PA 14, HP 1d6+5

Talent example
--------------
German:  Körperbeherrschung TaW 12; gelungene Probe, 5 TaP* übrig.
English: Body Control TP 12; successful Talent Test with 5 TP* remaining.

Spell example
-------------
German:  IGNIFAXIUS ZfW 14; 7 ZfP*; Kosten 8 AsP.
English: IGNIFAXIUS SP 14; 7 SP*; Cost 8 ASP.

Edition and canon note
----------------------
Wege des Schwerts contains substantially more weapons than the older English Basic Rules. Therefore,
many entries in this section are accurate descriptive translations rather than names attested in
the English core book. Proper Aventurian names are deliberately preserved to avoid inventing canon.

End of updated reference.
